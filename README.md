With the rapid expansion of urban settlements and increasing population density, the demand for reliable, efficient, and intelligent waste management systems has never been more critical. Among these, sewage systems play a pivotal role in maintaining urban sanitation and public health. However, conventional sewage systems are often prone to blockages due to the accumulation of debris, non-degradable waste, and sedimentation. These blockages can lead to overflows, foul odour emissions, infrastructure damage, and even the spread of hazardous diseases. The lack of real-time monitoring and proactive maintenance mechanisms further exacerbates these issues, making them difficult to detect until significant damage has occurred.    To address these challenges, the proposed system introduces a smart, IoT-enabled robotic solution for real-time detection and monitoring of sewage blockages. This system integrates a mobile bot equipped with a suite of sensors and modules to navigate through sewage pipelines, detect anomalies, and transmit critical data to a centralized monitoring interface.    At the core of the system is the NodeMCU ESP8266 microcontroller, which acts as the primary control unit for managing sensor data and wireless communication. The system leverages the ESP32-CAM module to capture live video footage from within the sewage lines, enabling visual inspection and blockage detection in real-time. To ensure the bot can move effectively through the pipelines, DC motors controlled by an L298N motor driver are utilized, mounted on wheels or tracks depending on terrain requirements.    For enhanced navigation and obstacle detection, the bot incorporates ultrasonic sensors (e.g., HC-SR04 or waterproof JSN-SR04T), along with IR sensors for line-following or wall detection within the pipes. A servo motor is used to control the orientation of the ESP32-CAM, allowing dynamic adjustment of the camera angle for improved visibility.    Environmental monitoring is facilitated through the integration of a gas sensor (MQ-135 or MQ-2) to detect harmful gases such as methane and hydrogen sulfide, along with a temperature and humidity sensor (DHT11/DHT22) to provide contextual environmental data.  A waterproof light source, such as LEDs or infrared LEDs, ensures sufficient illumination for the camera module in low-light or fully dark conditions.   The entire system is powered by a rechargeable battery pack (Li-ion/LiPo), managed by a 3S 12V BMS for safety and reliability. A voltage regulator (AMS1117) is used to maintain appropriate voltage levels for various components. For cases where cleaning may be required, the system includes a 12V/24V water pump, pipes, and an onboard water storage tank, enabling the bot to perform preliminary cleaning operations.    Data collected by the sensors is transmitted to a remote dashboard via the Blynk IoT platform or a local web server, allowing users to monitor live video feeds and sensor statistics, and receive alerts for any detected anomalies.   This project aims to present the design, implementation, and performance of the proposed IoT-based sewage blockage detection and monitoring system, highlighting its potential as a scalable solution for smart city infrastructure and urban sanitation management.
